## 13. Conventions the simulator fixes

Every quantity below has at least two conventions in the primary literature, and the research runs found published factor errors that trace to mixing them. The simulator adopts one convention per quantity, records the source it follows, restates it in the docstring of every module that touches the quantity, and pins the conversions with tests.
